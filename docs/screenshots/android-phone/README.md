# Android phone screenshots

Task 2026-11-10-20, part 2: the screens that have no phone mock-up yet. Taken on 10 November 2026 on an Android phone emulator (1280 × 2856 px at 480 dpi, so 427 × 952 dp), portrait, light theme, app and device locale in French.

- **Scale:** 1 dp = 1 px. Each image is the whole screen, Android's status bar and gesture bar included.
- **Data:** the logbook of the cloud test account. Its units are personalised: depth in feet, temperature in °C.
